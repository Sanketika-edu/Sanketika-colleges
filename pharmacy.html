Srinivasarao Pharmacy College(SRCP)

Advancing Healthcare Through Pharmaceutical Sciences

Srinivasarao Pharmacy College is committed to excellence in pharmaceutical education and research. We prepare students for diverse careers in healthcare, research, and the pharmaceutical industry.

Our curriculum integrates theoretical knowledge with practical laboratory skills, clinical exposure, and research opportunities.

Pharmacy Programs Offered

Bachelor of Pharmacy (B.Pharm)

Four-year undergraduate program covering pharmaceutical sciences, pharmacology, medicinal chemistry, and pharmacy practice.

Master of Pharmacy (M.Pharm)

Specialized postgraduate programs in Pharmaceuticals, Pharmacology, Pharmaceutical Chemistry, and Pharmacy Practice.

Doctor of Pharmacy (Pharm.D)

Six-year professional doctoral program focusing on clinical pharmacy, patient care, and therapeutic management.
