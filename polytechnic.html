Sanketika Polytechnic College

Practical Skills for Industrial Excellence

Sanketika Polytechnic College offers industry-focused diploma programs designed to equip students with practical skills and technical knowledge for immediate employment in various engineering sectors.

Our programs emphasize hands-on training, industry exposure, and project-based learning to create skilled technicians ready for the workforce.

Diploma Courses Offered

Diploma in Computer Engineering

Comprehensive training in construction technology, structural design, surveying, and project management.

Diploma in Electrical & Electronics Engineering

Focus on electrical circuits, power systems, electronics, and automation technology.

Diploma in Mechanical Engineering

Training in machine design, manufacturing processes, thermal engineering, and automotive technology.

Diploma in Civil engineering

Focus on computer hardware, software development, networking, and IT infrastructure.
