Sanketika Vidya Parishad (SVP) Engineering College

Innovation Through Technology and Research

Sanketika Engineering College offers cutting-edge undergraduate and postgraduate programs that blend theoretical foundations with practical applications. Our focus is on innovation, research, and industry readiness.

We provide state-of-the-art laboratories, industry collaborations, and research opportunities to prepare engineering leaders of tomorrow.

Engineering Programs Offered

Computer Science & Engineering (CSE)

Focus on algorithms, software development, artificial intelligence, data science, and computer systems.

Electronics & Communication Engineering (ECE)

Covers analog and digital electronics, communication systems, VLSI design, and embedded systems.

Mechanical Engineering (ME)

Study of mechanics, thermodynamics, manufacturing, robotics, and automotive systems.

Civil Engineering (CE)

Focus on structural engineering, construction management, environmental engineering, and transportation.

Information Technology (IT)

Covers web technologies, database management, networking, cybersecurity, and cloud computing.

M.Tech Programs

Postgraduate specializations in Advanced Manufacturing, VLSI Design, Computer Networks, Structural Engineering.
